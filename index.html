SelfMeter · AI-powered self-discovery

selfmeter.ir · Official websiteOpen in Telegram

Built an AI-powered self-discovery platform with a large language model (LLM) interpretation system that connects results across psychological and personality assessments. Delivered through a Telegram Bot and React Mini App, it turns structured assessment evidence into integrated Persian reports, personality insights, and practical guidance.

  • Designed a multi-stage LLM pipeline: analyze → synthesize → render → verify, separating evidence analysis, cross-assessment synthesis, report composition, and output verification.
  • Prepared anonymized evidence packets from eligible assessment results and enforced structured outputs, grounding, and semantic validation to keep interpretations tied to the supplied evidence.
  • Implemented controlled retries and auditable stage, model-call, and artifact records for tracing generation and diagnosing failures. The interpretation system supports personal and compatibility insights with non-diagnostic framing.
  • Ran interpretation jobs asynchronously with Celery and Redis, backed by Django/DRF and MariaDB, and delivered summaries and detailed reports through the React Mini App.
